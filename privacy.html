1Introduction

Piwi ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Piwi mobile application (the "App").

By using Piwi, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Information from Third Parties

If you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture (optional)

We use this information only to pre-fill your profile and authenticate you. We do not access any other Google data.

If you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and, depending on your choice, your name and either your real email address or a private relay email address generated by Apple. If you choose to hide your email, we only see the relay address. We use this information only to create and authenticate your account. When you delete your account, we revoke the Sign in with Apple connection.

What We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect:

  • GPS location or precise geolocation
  • Phone contacts or address book
  • Phone number
  • Camera access (we use the system photo picker only)
  • Microphone access
  • Health data beyond race finish times
  • Financial or payment information

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we collect and store a device push token (issued by Expo, our push delivery provider) linked to your account. This token is used only to deliver notifications about your results, follows, and other account activity. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and, depending on your device, Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android). You can disable push notifications at any time in Settings, which clears the stored token from our servers. In-app notification content (e.g. "your result was approved," "someone followed you") is stored linked to your account so you can view your notification history within the App.

Analytics

We use PostHog (EU-hosted) to measure screen views and feature usage. We do not use session recording, and we do not use this data for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Analytics.

Reporting and Blocking

If you report a user or content, or block a user, we store the report (including the reason you provide) and the block, linked to your account. Reports are sent by email to Piwi moderators so they can review them. The person you report is not told who reported them. Blocks are used to hide that person's content from you and to hide your content from them.

Guest Mode

You can browse parts of the App without creating an account by using Guest Mode. While in Guest Mode, you are not identified by name or email and we do not create a profile for you; your guest status is stored only on your device. Like any app that loads content from a server, our servers and providers receive technical data such as your IP address when the App requests races or leaderboards, and anonymous usage analytics apply unless you have turned them off. If you later create an account, this local guest state is cleared and normal account data collection (as described above) begins.

6Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Password hashing: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms by Supabase Auth.
  • Row-level security: Database rules ensure you can only create, modify, or delete your own data. Some data — such as approved race results, leaderboard rankings, and follow relationships — is intentionally viewable by other users or the public, as described elsewhere in this policy.
  • Private evidence storage: Evidence photos submitted with result claims are stored in a private location accessible only to you and to Piwi administrators reviewing your claim — they are never publicly accessible, regardless of your claim's status.
  • Image compression: Evidence photos are resized to 1200px width and compressed to 80% quality before storage. Profile avatar photos are resized to 400px width and compressed to 80% quality before storage.
  • Password protection: Supabase Auth stores passwords as hashes. Other profile and app data is handled under the access controls described above.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:
1. Go to Settings → Delete Account
2. Confirm the deletion
3. Your account and personal data are deleted immediately

When you delete your account:

  • Your profile details are erased, and any record that must remain is anonymized so it can no longer be linked to you
  • Your result claims and evidence photos are removed
  • Your login credentials are permanently deleted, and you can register again later with the same email
  • Your wishlist, follows, blocks, reports you filed, notifications and push token are deleted
  • If you signed in with Apple, the Sign in with Apple connection is revoked

Note: Race results already published to public leaderboards may be retained in anonymized form that no longer identifies you.
